        What this does

        The search builds a model of your series from a handful of pieces (a smooth trend, a straight line, a repeating cycle, noise, a change of regime) and keeps the combination that explains the data best for its size.

        Each piece then gets a sentence, a chart and a check. Tell it what you already know (a weekly cycle, the date of a promotion) and it reports what it found against that.
